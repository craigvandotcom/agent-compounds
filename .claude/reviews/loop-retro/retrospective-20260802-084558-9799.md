## Ablation Verdict (this run only)

Loop-lite (intent+contracts+gates) held quality parity with the full ac-loop skill on this run:
zero Critical/High findings survived review, beads-closed-gate and lint.sh stayed green
throughout (0 trips on both), and the deterministic backstops (dcg, pre-commit guard,
conductor's independent artifact re-verification per delegation-contract clause 4) caught what
needed catching without the full skill's longer phase prose loaded. What it visibly cost: 5
category-3 findings (4 already beaded: ac-kb8, ac-qsz, ac-vqf, ac-jj0, plus the un-beaded `br`
tty flake above) surfaced only because canon the full skill's prose would have spelled out
in-line (refine/beadify payloads, the TaskCreate fallback, the stale-mark ladder, a hardcoded
path, a tty quirk) was compressed out of the lite contract — each cost a child a self-correction
or an ad hoc improvisation, evidenced concretely by the 4x-independent-TaskCreate-rediscovery
figure. None of these were quality escapes (everything self-corrected in-run, 0 gate exit
failures, review still caught the one real defect the batch itself introduced). The ablation's
saving — a materially shorter contract and a ~4-minute orient — came at the cost of small,
repeated child-side friction rather than any output-quality regression this run.
